The short version

Stay at Park Hyatt Kyoto. It sits on Ninenzaka in Higashiyama, so the best night-lit cherry trees (Maruyama Park, Kiyomizu-dera, Kodai-ji) are a short walk away, and the Yasaka Pagoda is outside the window. Book cash direct on hyatt.com, on a refundable rate, now: there are no points or award nights for these dates, but Globalist benefits apply to a paid Hyatt rate.

April 1–4 is a good bet: in a normal year Kyoto hits full bloom around April 4, and full bloom lasts about a week.

Suggested back-to-back trip: Tokyo, then Kyoto

Tokyo Sat Mar 27 (or Sun Mar 28) → Thu Apr 1, then the Shinkansen to Kyoto for Apr 1 → Sun Apr 4. Add Mon Apr 5 if you want one more peak day in Kyoto. See the Tokyo cherry blossom guide for hotels and spots there.

Why this order: Tokyo blooms first. In the Japan Meteorological Agency's normal years (1991–2020 average), full bloom is Mar 31 in Tokyo and Apr 4 in Kyoto, and full bloom lasts about a week. Tokyo first catches Tokyo's normal peak, and moving to Kyoto on Apr 1 lands you there as it peaks.

Recent years have often run earlier than normal (4 of the last 6 in Kyoto). In an early year, Tokyo may be past peak by the end of March while Kyoto is at its best Apr 1–4; in a late year like 2024, Kyoto peaks around Apr 5, which is why the Apr 5 extension helps. Thu Apr 1: Tokyo → Kyoto

  1. Wed Mar 31, evening: forward your suitcasesHand them to your Tokyo hotel's front desk for Yamato TA-Q-BIN (takkyubin) to your Kyoto hotel. Tokyo to Kyoto normally arrives the next day, so send them the day before you travel. Limit: 200 cm (length + width + height) and 30 kg per bag.
  2. Morning: Nozomi from Tokyo StationThe fastest Nozomi trains take about 2 h 11 min to 2 h 15 min to Kyoto, every few minutes. Reserve seats ahead; the 9–10 am trains put you in Kyoto around lunchtime. A bag over 160 cm total needs a reserved “oversized baggage” seat (no extra charge), which is another reason to forward luggage.
  3. Kyoto Station → hotelTaxi from the Hachijo (south) or Karasuma (north) exit; Higashiyama hotels are roughly 15 minutes away (approx). Drop your day bag, then follow the Apr 1 plan below.
  4. Same-day optionIf you'd rather not send bags the day before, JR Central and JTB's LUGGAGE EXPRESS moves bags from select Tokyo hotels to select Kyoto hotels the same day (drop by 9 am; ¥13,200 per bag by 9 pm, ¥16,500 by 6 pm, per its launch notice).

Keep everything refundable. Official 2027 bloom forecasts start in winter (roughly December to February) and only get precise in March, so book refundable hotel rates on both legs and buy Shinkansen tickets closer to the date (JR seat reservations open about a month ahead).

A 3-day sakura plan

Built around Park Hyatt, arriving from Tokyo by Shinkansen on Apr 1 (see the suggested trip above). Go early (before 8 am) or after dark, when the crowds thin out. Night light-up dates below are from 2026; 2027 dates are announced each March.

Thu Apr 1: arrive from Tokyo and stay close

  1. Morning Nozomi from Tokyo, taxi to the hotelAbout 2 h 15 min by train, then roughly 15 minutes by taxi from Kyoto Station (approx). Suitcases sent by takkyubin the day before should be waiting; leave bags at the desk if the room isn't ready.
  2. Check in (3 pm) and stroll Nene-no-Michi to Kodai-jiA 5-minute walk; Kodai-ji's weeping cherry tends to bloom a little before most trees.
  3. Sunset drinks at KohakuThe hotel bar faces the Yasaka Pagoda; opens at 5 pm.
  4. Dinner at YasakaTeppanyaki chef's course with pagoda views; 20 seats, book ahead.
  5. Maruyama Park after dark10-minute walk to the lit weeping cherry (2026: 6–10 pm through Apr 8).

Fri Apr 2: eastern Kyoto by morning

  1. 7 am taxi to Keage InclineAbout 10 minutes (approx); walk the tracks before tour groups arrive.
  2. Philosopher's Path, south to northAbout 2 km of canal and cherry trees; allow an hour with stops (approx).
  3. Heian Shrine gardenPink weeping cherries around a pond; a short taxi from the path.
  4. Afternoon at the hotel spaRest up for the evening.
  5. Shirakawa at dusk, then kaisekiWalk the canal, then dinner at Kikunoi Honten or Kyoyamato (see Where to eat).

Sat Apr 3: Arashiyama by the river

  1. Early taxi to Arashiyama35–45 minutes (approx). Togetsukyo Bridge and the bamboo grove are quiet before 8 am.
  2. Hozu River boat rideA 16 km ride downriver from Kameoka to Arashiyama, about 2 hours (take the train up first). Or rent a rowboat below the bridge.
  3. Kaiseki lunch at Kitcho ArashiyamaBook as early as they allow.
  4. Nijo Castle SAKURA NIGHTS on the way back2026: Mar 19–Apr 19, 6–10 pm, ticketed; lit cherry trees and palace interiors.

Sun Apr 4: one last early walk

  1. Kiyomizu-dera at openingIt opens at 6 am, a 10–15 minute walk; Ninenzaka and Sannenzaka are nearly empty then.
  2. Slow breakfast, then check out by noonPark Hyatt checkout is 12 pm.
If it rains. Light rain is fine and the crowds drop. Swap days so the Arashiyama trip lands on the driest day, and keep a long kaiseki lunch, the spa, or Nijo Castle's palace interiors for the wet one. Heavy rain and wind can strip petals, which is the main reason to keep one day flexible.

Where to eat

  • Yasaka, Park Hyatt: teppanyaki chef's course facing the pagoda, dinner 5:30–9:30 pm.
  • Kohaku, Park Hyatt: cocktail bar for the sunset pagoda view, 5 pm to midnight.
  • Kyoyamato, beside Park Hyatt: kaiseki restaurant dating to 1877, with a teahouse overlooking the city.
  • Kikunoi Honten: famous Michelin-starred kaiseki, a short walk from the hotel near Maruyama Park.
  • Kitcho Arashiyama: Michelin-starred kaiseki for the Arashiyama day.
  • SoNoMa at Capella Kyoto: a 12-seat restaurant from the team behind SingleThread.

Michelin star counts change each year, so check the current guide when booking.

Good to know

  • Weather: April averages roughly 20°C (68°F) highs and 9°C (48°F) lows; early April nights feel chilly.
  • Wear layers, a light coat, and comfortable shoes for steep stone lanes.
  • Crowds peak on weekends; Apr 3–4, 2027 is a Saturday and Sunday.
  • In Higashiyama, walking is often faster than a taxi stuck in traffic. Use taxis for Arashiyama, Daigo-ji and early mornings.
  • Kyoto's petals fall about a week after full bloom, so keep plans loose until the March forecasts.
